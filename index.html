Our story

Little things.
Happy home.

Home is where you slow down, show off a little, and make memories. We think the things that fill it should feel just as good.

So we're building Vimekiro around a simple idea: pieces that look great, hold up season after season, and don't cost a boutique price. No fuss. No hype. Just things you'll be glad you brought home.

Made for the way you live.

Questions

Good to know.

What is Vimekiro?

Vimekiro is a new American home and garden brand. We make thoughtful pieces for living rooms, entryways, porches and gardens, with a boutique look at a fair price.

When do you open?

Our first collection is in the works. Join the list and we'll email you the day it's ready.

Where can I buy Vimekiro?

When we launch, you'll be able to shop Vimekiro online in the United States, in our own shop and on marketplaces like Amazon and Walmart.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We're starting in the United States.
